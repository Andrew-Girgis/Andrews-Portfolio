Problem Statement



             The rising cost of living is a global issue, influencing consumer
             behavior, supply chain dynamics, and economic stability.
             Key questions:
                 1   How does the rising cost of living impact supply chain stability
                     and costs?
                 2   What economic and regional factors contribute to cost
                     increases within supply chains?
                 3   How can organizations adapt supply chain strategies to
                     mitigate these effects?

Key Findings

             Increased cost of living destabilizes supply chains.
             Predictive models confirm reduced trade volumes with higher
